On the name. RadEcho exists because Weather Underground's RadBlast went dark around 2022 and never came back — a URL-parameterized NEXRAD viewer where you put a station or a lat/lon in and got an animated loop straight out. This is the room where the rebuild gets explained: what the radar is actually measuring, why a given decision was made, and what's still wrong with it. Blast radius, in the other sense, being roughly what you'd expect from changing something in the middle of a rendering pipeline.
